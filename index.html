Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mobile car wash and detailing service across Los Angeles.

What vehicles do you wash and detail?
We service all vehicles! Sedans, coupes, hatchbacks, electric vehicles (Teslas, Rivians, etc.), family crossovers, large SUVs, pickup trucks, work vans, as well as sports cars and luxury exotics. We have customized equipment and formulas for all paint finishes and interior materials.
Do you need access to water or electricity at my home or building?
No, our mobile vans are 100% self-contained! We carry our own filtered deionized water tanks (0-PPM TDS for spot-free drying) and quiet commercial power generators. We can detail your vehicle on the street, in a driveway, parking lot, or underground garage without hooking up to your utilities.
Can you wash inside apartment parking garages like The Orsini?
Yes! Because our headquarters are located directly at The Orsini Apartments (550 N Figueroa St), we are specialized in zero-runoff dry-steam and rinseless washing techniques that are 100% compliant with underground garage rules and HOA policies.
Are you truly open and available 24 hours a day?
Yes. We operate round-the-clock 24/7. Whether you need an early morning wash before work, an overnight turnaround while you sleep, or a late-night emergency spill cleanup, our dispatch team can be reached anytime at +1 346-377-3170.
How does your custom quote work?
Every vehicle is unique in size and condition. Simply let us know your vehicle make/model and desired package (or text us photos of your car to +1 346-377-3170), and we will provide an instant, upfront custom quote with zero surprise fees.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zelle, Venmo, and Cash.
